# Third-party brand assets

Check Make displays application icons for Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer, UltiMaker Cura, and Creality Print solely to help users recognize compatible export targets.

The names, icons, and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Their appearance in Check Make does not imply sponsorship or endorsement. The raster icons under `public/slicer-icons/` were derived from the corresponding application bundles installed on macOS and should be reviewed for distribution rights before a public binary release.
