# Check Make GUI concept v1 — generation prompt

Built-in image generation was used with the supplied screenshot as a composition and visual-density reference only.

## Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup

Asset type: high-fidelity non-functional desktop application GUI concept for the Check Make project

Primary request: Design a polished full-window macOS desktop UI for Check Make, a 3D-print preparation assistant, based on its current version 0.2 capabilities.

Scene and layout: One clean macOS app window with a native dark title bar and light cool-gray application canvas. Use a narrow left navigation, a large central 3D viewport, and a structured right decision panel. Follow the supplied reference's three-column clarity without copying it pixel-for-pixel.

Brand: Use Check Make's warm white and cool canvas surfaces, technical ink text, restrained blueprint navy, Make Green `#35C98B` highlights, and Action Green `#168866` for accessible actions. Include a small checked-cube symbol next to the app name.

Left navigation: “Overview”, “Analysis”, “Geometry”, “Uncertainty”, “Settings”, and “Export”, with Overview selected.

Central viewport: Show a realistic gray mounting bracket or functional mechanical part on a dark gridded printer build plate with subtle XYZ axes and clean studio lighting. Show “mounting-bracket.stl”, segmented controls “Selected”, “Risk map”, and “Compare”, plus the geometry stats “62.4 mm height”, “4 mesh parts”, and “3 overhang regions”.

Right panel: Show “Recommendation”, the status “Review required”, “Ready after 2 decisions”, a compact green progress ring without a fabricated numeric readiness score, and “Top recommendations” containing “Use PETG for outdoor exposure”, “Rotate to strongest orientation”, and “Add supports in 2 regions”. Show the assumption “Intended load direction needs confirmation”. Show “Key settings” with “0.20 mm layers”, “4 walls”, and “25% gyroid infill”, followed by “Review recommendation”. Under “Export project”, show “Core 3MF”, “Bambu Studio”, “OrcaSlicer”, “PrusaSlicer”, “UltiMaker Cura”, and “Creality Print”.

Bottom evidence strip: “Local geometry measured”, “Rules are evidence-linked”, and “No structural simulation”.

Style: Crisp premium Figma-quality product mockup, modern native macOS utility, realistic UI screenshot, coherent iconography, careful spacing, subtle borders and shadows, and highly legible typography.

Constraints: Show only one app window. Visually communicate evidence, uncertainty, and review before export. This is a static concept with no cursor or annotations outside the window.

Avoid: Mobile layout, browser chrome, dark theme, neon gradients, fake charts, generic dashboard clutter, invented capabilities, FEM claims, mesh-repair claims, marketing slogans, and watermarks.
